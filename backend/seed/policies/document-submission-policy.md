# Document Submission Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to every new joiner at Azentio. Required documents depend on role, department, grade and work location. Your personalised checklist in the onboarding portal lists exactly which documents apply to you.

## Documents required for all employees
- A government-issued photo ID: passport, Aadhaar card or driver's licence. The ID must be valid (not expired) on your joining date.
- Address proof dated within the last 3 months: utility bill, rental agreement or Aadhaar card.
Both must be submitted at least 10 days before the joining date.

## India-specific documents
Employees based in India (Bengaluru, Pune, Mumbai, Hyderabad) must also submit:
- PAN card, required for payroll tax registration, at least 10 days before joining.
- A cancelled cheque or bank letter for salary credit, at least 5 days before joining.

## Role and grade-specific documents
- Engineering roles specify a minimum qualification, so the highest education certificate (degree or provisional certificate) is required.
- Lateral hires at grade G3 and above must provide a relieving or experience letter from their previous employer, at least 7 days before joining. Interns and grades G1–G2 are exempt.

## Accepted formats
Documents must be PDF, JPG or PNG files under 10 MB. Scans must be legible, with all four corners visible. Password-protected PDFs are not accepted.

## How to submit
Reply to your welcome email with the documents attached, or upload them in the onboarding portal. Each document is checked automatically and then by the People Team.

## Rejections and corrections
A document is sent back for correction if it is expired, illegible, the wrong file type, or if the name or date of birth does not match your offer details. You will receive an email explaining the reason. Please re-submit within 2 working days.
