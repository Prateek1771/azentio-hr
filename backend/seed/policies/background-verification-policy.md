# Background Verification Policy

## Purpose
Azentio verifies identity, education and employment history for all new joiners before granting access to company systems.

## When it starts
Background verification (BGV) starts automatically once your identity, address, education and experience documents have been accepted. It typically takes 3–5 working days.

## What is checked
- Identity and address, using your submitted documents.
- Education, with the issuing institution (Engineering roles).
- Previous employment, using your relieving letter (grade G3 and above).

## Impact on access
System access (development tools, CRM, production systems) is granted only after BGV is complete. Your laptop and email can be provisioned before BGV completes.

## Owner
BGV is run by the Compliance team. Questions go to the Compliance desk through HR.
