# US Onboarding Policy

## Applicability
This applies to employees based in the United States (New York, Austin).

## Form I-9
US federal law requires employment eligibility verification. Submit Form I-9 supporting documents (for example, a US passport, or a driver's licence plus a social security card) within 3 business days of your start date.

## Form W-4
Submit a signed Form W-4 at least 3 days before joining so federal tax withholding can be set up.

## Benefits enrolment
Enrol in medical, dental and 401(k) plans within 30 days of joining through the benefits portal.
