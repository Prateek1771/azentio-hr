# Information Security Policy

## Acknowledgement
Every employee must read and acknowledge this policy before being granted access to production systems or customer data. Acknowledge it in your onboarding portal, at least 1 day before joining.

## Key rules
- Never share passwords. Use the company password manager and enable MFA on all accounts.
- Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage.
- Lock your screen when away. Laptops use full-disk encryption.
- Report suspected phishing or security incidents to security@azentio.com within 1 hour.
- Use only approved software from the company software catalogue.

## Consequences
Violations may lead to disciplinary action, up to termination.
