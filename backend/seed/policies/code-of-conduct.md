# Code of Conduct

## Acknowledgement
All employees must acknowledge the Code of Conduct in the onboarding portal on or before their first day.

## Principles
- Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.
- Avoid conflicts of interest, and declare outside employment or investments in competitors.
- Gifts above INR 5,000 (USD 60) must be declared to HR.
- Raise concerns through your manager, HR or the anonymous ethics hotline.
