# First Day Guide

## Arrival
Report to reception at 9:30 AM local time on your joining date. Bring the original of your photo ID for verification.

## Office addresses
- Bengaluru: Azentio Tech Park, Outer Ring Road, Bellandur.
- Mumbai: One BKC, Bandra Kurla Complex.
- Pune: Panchshil Business Park, Viman Nagar.
- New York: 1 World Trade Center, 85th Floor.

## Day-one agenda
- 9:30: Badge and desk allocation (Facilities).
- 10:00: Orientation session with HR: company overview, benefits and policies.
- 12:30: Team lunch with your manager.
- 14:00: Laptop handover and account setup with IT.

## Dress code
Business casual.

## Who to contact
Your People Team partner (HR) for anything about onboarding. Your manager for team and role questions.
