# IT Provisioning Guide

## Sequence
1. Your HR record is created once your identity and payroll documents are accepted.
2. After the HR record exists, IT is automatically asked for a laptop and a corporate email account.
3. Role-specific access is granted after background verification and the information security acknowledgement are complete.

## Laptop
Engineering roles receive a MacBook Pro (16GB+). All other roles receive a standard business laptop. Laptops are handed over at the office on day one, or shipped for remote employees.

## Email and accounts
Your corporate email is firstname.lastname@azentio.com. Login details are shared with your personal email the day before joining.

## Role-specific access
- Engineering: GitHub, Jira and AWS (non-production) after BGV and the security acknowledgement. Production access needs a separate manager approval after 30 days.
- Sales: Salesforce CRM after the email account and BGV are ready.

## Owner
IT Service Desk. Typical turnaround is 2 working days from the request.
